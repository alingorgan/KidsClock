What to try

  • The app opens on the list of routines. Two examples are provided (Evening, Morning). Tap one to preview its activities, then Start.
  • Edit a routine: rename it, add, remove or move activities, change a picture, colour, minutes or who starts it. Save is blocked, with the reason shown, until the routine is valid.
  • "+ New routine" starts empty. Duplicate copies a routine so you can tweak it. Delete asks twice.
  • Reload the page: your routines are still there. A run in progress is not kept; it starts again from the beginning (decision 31).
  • Sound is set per routine. Age and progress colour apply to every routine.
  • On the child screen, press and hold the small dot top-left for about a second to open the grown-up sheet. "Back to routines" leaves the run.
  • Quick timer: "5 more min" extends this activity; "Something else now" pauses it and it resumes 5 s after the quick timer ends. From the list, "Quick timer" runs one with no routine.
