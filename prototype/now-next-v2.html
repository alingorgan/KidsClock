What to try

  • Tap the circle in the middle. With a photo or clip it opens up, plays, then closes by itself.
  • Press and hold the small dot in the top-left for about a second to open the grown-up sheet.
  • Let the timer run out. Bath time waits for the grown-up to unlock it; story time can only be started from the sheet.
  • Switch to "Under 3" in setup: only line pictures, and "next" stays hidden until the grown-up shows it.
  • In setup, compare progress colours (whole screen, ring, none) and the end sound. Sound starts after your first tap.
  • Quick timer: press and hold the dot. "5 more min" extends this activity, before it ends or once it is red. "Something else now" (Playground, snack...) pauses this activity; when that timer ends the chime plays, the screen goes red, and 5 seconds later the paused activity picks up where it left off. In setup, "Quick timer" runs one with no routine.
  • Run the routine to the end: "Sleep time" has no chime, and the screen fades to dark over 5 seconds.
  • Your setup choices (minutes, who starts what, colour, sound, age) are remembered on this device. Photos are not.
